How to order

  1. Pick your flowers on the flowers page. Each card shows the price and how many are in stock. Add the ones you like to your cart.
  2. Open your cart, check how many of each, and fill in the order form: who gets the flowers, the day, a slot (morning, afternoon or evening) and how you pay: UPI, card or COD. Add a card message like Happy birthday, Maa!
  3. We deliver fresh, wrapped flowers on the day and in the slot you chose.

Your cart and your orders are saved in this browser, so they are still there next time. Sign in to see your past orders on My orders.
