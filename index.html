Questions and answers

Where do you deliver, and what does it cost?

We deliver across Jalandhar, Phagwara and Ludhiana. Delivery is ₹50 per order and free on orders of ₹999 or more. You can order for today or any day in the next 60 days.

How can I pay?

Choose UPI, card or cash on delivery when you place the order. You see the full total, with delivery, before you confirm.

How do I keep my flowers fresh?

Cut 2 cm off each stem at a slant and put them straight into clean, cool H2O. Change the water every 2nd day and keep the vase out of direct sun. Keep cut flowers away from ripe fruit, which makes them wilt faster.

How do I contact you?

Call, email or drop in at the shop. Our phone number, email and address are on the contact page.
