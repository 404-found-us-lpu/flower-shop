Price calculator

This calculator has no buttons. It is a small JavaScript program that lives in your browser's Console. You type a short command, press Enter, and it tells you what your flowers will cost, delivery included.

Use a laptop or desktop. Phone browsers do not have a Console.

1. Open the Console

  1. Press F12 on this page.
    Or Ctrl + Shift + J. On a Mac: Cmd + Option + J.
  2. Click the Console tab if it is not already open.
  3. You will see a welcome message and a sample bill.
  4. Click after the > sign, type a command and press Enter.

2. Type a command

bill(id, qty)
The price of qty flowers of one kind (1 to 10), with delivery. The flower id is its number in the list at the bottom of this page.
cheapest()
The lowest-priced flower in the shop.
byType("Plants")
Every flower of one type: Bouquets, Indian favourites, Single stems, Plants or Gifts.
flowers
The whole list of 45 flowers with their id, name, price and type.

3. Try these

Lines starting with > are what you type. The line under each one is the answer.

> bill(36, 4)
'Single Gerbera: 4 x Rs 25 + Rs 50 delivery = Rs 150'

> bill(12, 2)
'Birthday Combo: 2 x Rs 599 + free delivery = Rs 1198'

> bill(7)
'Single Red Rose: 1 x Rs 29 + Rs 50 delivery = Rs 79'

> cheapest()
'Cheapest: Single Gerbera at Rs 25'

> byType("Gifts")
(4) [{…}, {…}, {…}, {…}]

> bill(50, 1)
'There is no flower with id 50. Use an id from 1 to 45.'

bill(7) leaves out the quantity, so it uses 1. After byType, click the small arrow to open the list.

How the bill is worked out

  1. Subtotal = price of one flower × quantity.
  2. Delivery = Rs 50, or free when the subtotal is Rs 999 or more.
  3. Total = subtotal + delivery.

The calculator only works out prices. To place an order, use the order page.
