How to order

  1. Pick your flowers on the flowers page. Each card shows the price and how many are in stock.
  2. Work out the price if you like. The price calculator adds the Rs 50 delivery charge, or takes it off from Rs 999.
  3. Fill in the order form: who gets the flowers, the day, a slot (morning, afternoon or evening) and how you pay: UPI, card or COD. Add a card message like Happy birthday, Maa!
  4. We deliver fresh, wrapped flowers on the day and in the slot you chose.

Questions and answers

Where do you deliver, and what does it cost?

We deliver across Jalandhar, Phagwara and Ludhiana. Delivery is Rs 50 per order and free on orders of Rs 999 or more.

How can I pay?

Choose UPI, card or COD on the order form. With COD you pay in cash when the flowers arrive.

How do I keep my flowers fresh?

Cut 2 cm off each stem at a slant and put them straight into clean, cool H2O. Change the water every 2nd day and keep the vase out of direct sun. Keep cut flowers away from ripe fruit, which makes them wilt faster.

How does the price calculator work?

Open the price calculator on a laptop, press F12 and click the Console tab. Then type a command like bill(14, 3) and press Enter to see the price of three marigolds with delivery.
